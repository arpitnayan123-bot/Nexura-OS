## Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: Mumbai Pilot (Q1 2026)

- Target: 3 hospitals, 10 clinics, 5 pharmacies
- Direct sales (founder-led)
- Free pilot → paid conversion
- Collect case studies + testimonials

### Phase 2: 4-City Expansion (Q3 2026)

- Cities: Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Chennai
- Sales team: 4 reps (1 per city)
- Channel partners: hospital associations, pharmacy chains
- Target: 50 hospitals, 200 clinics, 100 pharmacies

### Phase 3: National Scale (Q1 2027)

- 20 cities
- Self-serve onboarding for clinics + pharmacies
- Enterprise sales for hospitals
- Target: 500 hospitals, 2,000 clinics, 1,000 pharmacies

### Phase 4: Marketplace + International (Q3 2027)

- Open AI API for third-party developers
- SE Asia expansion (Singapore, UAE, Thailand)
- Target: $10M ARR

## Competitive Moats

1. **Unified ecosystem**: 7 products sharing one patient identity. Switching cost increases over time.
2. **AI-native**: Every product has AI features built-in. Competitors can't retrofit AI easily.
3. **Compliance**: ABDM + NABH + DPDP + CDSCO + IRDAI + GST built-in from day one.
4. **Blood Test at Home**: Phlebotomist network + AI interpretation. No competitor has this.
5. **Patient identity**: ABHA-linked across all products. Data compounds over time.
6. **Indian-optimized**: Built for Indian workflows, Indian languages, Indian regulations.
