# Nexura OS — Seed Round Pitch

## The One-Liner
Nexura OS is the unified, AI-native operating system for Indian healthcare — connecting hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, and 1.4 billion patients through one warm, intelligent ecosystem.

## The Problem
70% of Indian healthcare runs on paper. There is no unified patient identity. Doctors write prescriptions by hand. Pharmacies track stock in notebooks. Patients carry physical files across hospitals. The system is fragmented, invisible, and broken.

**Market size:**
- TAM: $372B (Indian healthcare spend)
- SAM: $48B (digital health + hospital IT)
- SOM (5yr): $2.1B (5% SAM penetration)

**The ABDM tailwind:** The government is forcing digitization via the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission. 680M+ ABHA IDs created. 2026 is the mandatory compliance year. This is a forced market creation.

## The Solution
Seven products, one platform, zero friction:

1. **Hospital OS** — 18-module HMS (OPD, IPD, OT, EHR, Nursing, Lab, Radiology, Blood Bank, Billing, Insurance, AI Assistant). ABDM + NABH compliant.
2. **Clinic OS** — HealthPlix-style EMR with SOAP consultation, drug autocomplete, ABHA registry.
3. **Pharmacia** — AI pharmacy POS with voice billing, prescription OCR, GST e-invoice, Schedule H register.
4. **Patient Portal** — Unified health record + Blood Test at Home with AI report interpretation.
5. **Connect** — Doctor-patient chat, voice, video across all products.
6. **Know Your Health** — 15 AI tools (symptom checker, derma scan, X-ray reader, diet planner).
7. **Global** — Medical tourism discovery for international patients.

## Why Now
1. **ABDM mandate** — Government forcing digitization. 680M ABHA IDs. 2026 compliance deadline.
2. **AI maturity** — LLMs (GLM-4-Plus) + VLMs can now do clinical tasks at scale.
3. **DPDP 2023** — Data privacy law creates demand for compliant platforms.
4. **Post-COVID** — Telemedicine adoption jumped from 10% to 75% in India.
5. **Smartphone penetration** — 750M+ smartphones in India. Patients are ready.
